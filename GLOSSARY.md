# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this tool means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term.

Write each entry as a `### Term` heading, then one or two plain sentences, then an optional `Not:` line listing words that must not be used for it. A number a rule decides is a `{placeholder}` the core fills, so the glossary cannot disagree with the rules. Keep entries in alphabetical order. `crates/core/tests/copy.rs` checks the format, the placeholders and the `Not:` words; `TONE.md` governs the prose.

### Box

The box of rounds in your pocket; taken out with the hand during a reload.

Not: magazine, clip

### Breaker

The handle on the fuse box on the court's west wall. Thrown with E, it puts out the court's lamps until someone throws it back, and the sudden dark leaves the cult blind for a moment. Shot, the box puts them out for good, and the cult hears the shot.

### Chamber

One of the six holes in the cylinder; each is empty, holds a round, or holds a spent case.

### Cultist

One of the robed figures chanting in the centre court. They see, hear, search and attack, and a shot drops one.

### Curio

A small thing left in a store that you can pick up with E and put down again: the toy, the camera, the music box and the telephone's receiver. Each answers once.

Not: item, collectible

### Cylinder

The part of the revolver that swings out to load and holds the six chambers.

Not: barrel, magazine

### Flashlight

The light in your left hand. It runs down; holding F and shaking the mouse charges it.

Not: torch

### Handle

The round handle of a stuck door. Hold the right button on it and draw circles with the mouse to turn it.

Not: knob, lever

### Hiding place

A booth or locker you can step into with E; the spirit passes you there unless it saw you go in.

### Little girl

The pale child a curio shows you. She is there for a moment and gone before you can reach her; she is not the spirit.

Not: ghost

### Planchette

The small heart-shaped piece of wood on the ouija board. Your hand rests on it while it slides to the letters.

Not: pointer, cursor

### Possession

The spirit inside a mannequin or the raffle car. A possessed thing moves only while you are not looking, and one hit drives the spirit out.

### Round

One cartridge for the revolver.

Not: bullet, ammo

### Spirit

The evil spirit that haunts the mall; it grows and hunts you after the ouija board answers.

Not: ghost, monster
